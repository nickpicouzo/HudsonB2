# HudsonB2
Autonomous Fixed Wing Flight-Test

David Gleason and I, Nicholas Picouzo, are Juniors at the University of Miami studying Mechanical Engineering with an Aerospace concentration. We're designing and building a custom 3D-printed fixed-wing aircraft to whether on-board sensing can tell us how far the aircraft is left or right of a marked runway, even when the nose is pointed away from it.

Our first experiment is a fixture that lets us adjust the camera position independently. We'll then compare the software's estimate against camera positions before in-flight testing. During our first test-flights the computer vision will record estimates, not control the aircraft.
